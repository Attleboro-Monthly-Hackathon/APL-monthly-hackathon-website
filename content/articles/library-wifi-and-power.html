The community room has tables, chairs, and projector access. Power strips are available near the walls; ask an organizer if you need an extension cord.

Connectivity

Join the guest Wi‑Fi network posted near the entrance. Speeds are good for documentation, GitHub, and light development. Large downloads may be slower — cache what you can before you arrive.

Focus tips

  • Sit near natural light in the morning; the west windows glow later in the day.
  • Use headphones when watching tutorials.
  • Step into the hallway for calls so the room stays calm.

The meeting room has tables, chairs, and projector access. Power strips are available near the walls; ask an organizer or a librarian if you need an extension cord.

Connectivity

Join the Attleboro Public Library guest Wi‑Fi posted near the entrance. Speeds are good for documentation, GitHub, and light development. Large downloads may be slower — cache what you can before you arrive.

Focus tips

  • Arrive a little early to settle in before the opening circle.
  • Use headphones when watching tutorials.
  • Step into the hallway for calls so the room stays calm.

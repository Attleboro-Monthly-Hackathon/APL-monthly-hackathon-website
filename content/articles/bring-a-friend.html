Walking into a room full of laptops can feel intimidating. Bringing a friend turns the day into a shared adventure instead of a solo leap.

How to invite someone

  • Frame it as a Saturday at the Attleboro Public Library, not a competition.
  • Offer to work on something small together — a webpage, a quiz, a playlist tool.
  • Promise you can leave early if it is not their vibe (it usually is).

If your friend has never written code, start with Your First HTML Page before the meetup, or do it together on site.

Find out which houses in your neighborhood are taking part in the festivities and rewarding trick-or-treaters.

This Attleboro-focused workshop is a local map and community-data project: collect participating candy houses, show them on a map, and help families plan a walking route.

Light experience

  • A simple map of participating houses (markers + address or nickname).
  • A printable block-by-block list for a single neighborhood.
  • A form that stores new houses in localStorage for the session.

Heavy experience

  • Routing between houses (shortest walk, avoid busy roads).
  • Hours, accessibility notes, and “porch light on” status.
  • A shareable link so neighbors can update the map together.

Helpful resources

  • Leaflet quick start
  • OpenStreetMap
  • Data for Good for other local-map ideas

Starter project ideas

  • A simple map of free water fountains or quiet study spots.
  • A walking tour webpage for a historic block near 74 North Main Street.
  • A “lost & found places” form that stores tips in localStorage.
  • A printable scavenger hunt of Attleboro landmarks.

Helpful resources

  • OpenStreetMap and Leaflet for beginner-friendly maps
  • Public GIS open data portals for local datasets
  • Attleboro open data and the library’s local history collections for story prompts

Themes are optional. If you already have a project, keep going — and share what you made at the end of the day.

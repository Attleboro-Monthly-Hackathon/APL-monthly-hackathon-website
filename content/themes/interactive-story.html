Theme: Creative coding

Why it works: Merges writing, design, and logic. Great for mixed groups—storytellers and programmers can pair up.

Light experience

Build a choose-your-own-adventure with branching paths, at least two endings, and a little state (inventory, flags, or score).

Project ideas

  1. Twine story — Passages, links, and variables for inventory or relationships; export to HTML.
  2. HTML/JS adventure — One page app: show scene text, choices as buttons, track inventory in memory or localStorage.
  3. Mystery with clues — Collect items that unlock later dialogue options.
  4. Classroom / library setting story — Set the adventure in a familiar place so peers recognize it instantly.

Learning goals

  • Branching narrative and state
  • Separating content (story text) from logic (what unlocked what)
  • Simple UI for reading and choosing

Heavy experience

Go beyond branching text: generative visuals, sound reactivity, or a short visual novel with saves.

Project ideas

  1. Microphone-reactive generative art — p5.js sketch where shapes, color, or particle motion respond to mic amplitude/frequency.
  2. Visual novel — Scenes with backgrounds, character sprites, dialogue boxes, choices, and save/load slots.
  3. Procedural poetry / art — Grammar or noise-based generator that still feels authored (seeded randomness).
  4. Hybrid — Twine/Ink story that opens a p5.js sketch at key moments, or a novel that changes art based on player stats.

Learning goals

  • Creative coding toolchains (p5.js, assets, fonts)
  • Audio input and FFT / amplitude (where relevant)
  • Save systems and scene management
  • Performance basics (don’t redraw more than you need)

Writing tips for coders

  • Outline endings first, then work backward to the choices that lead there.
  • Keep early passages short so testers can reach a branch quickly.
  • One inventory item or flag is enough to make the story feel “smart.”

Suggested session flow

  1. Write a 3-branch outline on paper (start → mid → 2–3 endings).
  2. Implement navigation before fancy styling or art.
  3. Add one stateful mechanic (key item, trust meter, or save).
  4. Playtest with a partner for 5 minutes; fix the confusing choice labels.
